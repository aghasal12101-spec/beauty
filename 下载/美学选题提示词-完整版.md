# 美学账号选题 · GPT Image 提示词合集


## 使用说明（GPT Image 版）

本套提示词已针对 GPT Image（ChatGPT 生图或 OpenAI 图像 API）改写：去掉了 Midjourney 的 `--ar`、`--style`、`--no` 等参数，改成完整的自然语言描述。方括号 `[ ]` 里是要替换的变量。

### GPT Image 与 Midjourney 的主要区别

| 事项 | GPT Image 的做法 |
| --- | --- |
| 画幅 | 写在提示词第一句，如 "A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper"。API 竖版可选 1024×1536（2:3），出图后裁成 9:16；ChatGPT 里直接说"竖版 9:16"即可 |
| 负面提示词 | 没有单独的负面栏，写成句子放在末尾："No text, no watermark, no border." |
| 风格统一 | 在同一个对话里连续生成；或上传第一张满意的图，加一句 "Match the style, color grading and lighting of the reference image." |
| 画面文字 | GPT Image 能较准确地画出文字。英文编号、标题可以直接让它画；中文建议 2–6 个字的短词，长句仍然后期加 |
| 局部修改 | 不用重新生成，直接在对话里说："Keep everything the same, but make the gold lines thinner." |
| 透明背景 | 表情包、贴纸可以要求 "transparent background"（API 设 `background: transparent`，输出 PNG） |
| 偏色 | 如果画面整体偏黄，末尾加 "neutral white balance, no warm color cast." |

### 通用结构

每条提示词按这个顺序写：**画幅与用途 → 主体 → 场景 → 光线 → 色彩 → 风格与镜头 → 构图留白 → 禁止项**。同一栏目内只换主体，其余部分固定，栏目的辨识度就来自这些固定部分。

### 画幅对应

| 用途 | 写进提示词的开头 | API 尺寸 | 构图提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 手机壁纸 | A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper | 1024×1536，再裁成 9:16 | 上方 1/3 留空给时钟 |
| 头像 | A square 1:1 profile picture | 1024×1024 | 主体居中，裁成圆形后仍完整 |
| 海报 / 小红书封面 | A vertical 3:4 poster | 1024×1536，再裁成 3:4 | 留出标题区 |

### 通用后期流程

1. 每个栏目选一张最满意的图作为"风格参考图"，之后每次生成都上传它。
2. 放大到 4K，套栏目专属调色预设。
3. 加 2–4% 颗粒噪点，削弱 AI 的塑料感。
4. 编号、标题位置和字体每个栏目固定。
5. 发布时勾选"AI 生成内容"标识。

### 动态版说明

GPT Image 只出静态图。需要动态壁纸或 GIF 时，先用 GPT Image 出静图，再交给可灵、即梦等图生视频工具。各栏目文件里的"动效提示词"就是给这一步用的。


## 01 镀金裂痕

栏目口号：**碎过的地方，会发光。** 每周一发布，编号 KIN-001 起。这个栏目卖的是情绪：破碎之后的修复与重生。画面是入口，文案才是涨粉点。

### 视觉规范

| 元素 | 规定 |
| --- | --- |
| 背景 | 纯黑 #0A0A0A，不出现任何场景 |
| 主体 | 白瓷 / 白大理石 / 石膏，色值约 #F2EEE6 |
| 金线 | 主金 #D4A84B，高光 #F3D88A；金线要"微微发光"，不要涂料感 |
| 光线 | 单侧 45° 硬光，另一侧沉入黑暗 |
| 构图 | 主体居中偏下，上方留空给时钟或文案 |
| 字体 | 英文 Cormorant Garamond，中文思源宋体 Light，金色小字 |

### 提示词

**壁纸 9:16**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A [white porcelain bowl] that was shattered and carefully repaired with kintsugi: glowing liquid-gold seams trace every crack, and a faint warm light seems to come from inside the gold lines. Pure black background with no scenery. A single hard light from the left at 45 degrees; the opposite side falls into deep shadow. Minimalist fine-art still life, museum quality, ultra-detailed porcelain texture, subtle film grain. Place the object in the lower half and leave the upper third completely black for the clock. Only 3 to 6 elegant cracks, not a dense web. No text, no watermark, no border.
```

**头像 1:1**

```
A square 1:1 profile picture. An extreme close-up of [a cracked white marble face] with eyes peacefully closed. Glowing gold kintsugi veins run across the cheek and forehead. Pure black background, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, centered composition so it still works when cropped into a circle. Serene and melancholic mood, macro fine-art photography. No text, no watermark.
```

**文案海报 3:4（可让 GPT Image 直接写编号）**

```
A vertical 3:4 poster. A [porcelain hand] with golden kintsugi cracks gently holding [a single white flower], placed in the lower half of the frame. Vast pure black negative space above. Soft gold rim light, elegant and quiet, fine-art still life photography with film grain. In the bottom-right corner, add a small, thin gold serif caption that reads exactly: "KIN-[001]". No other text, no watermark.
```

**动态版**（GPT Image 出静图后，丢进可灵 / 即梦图生视频）

```
Slow motion: molten gold slowly flows into the cracks of the porcelain, the gold lines light up one by one, camera static, black background, 4 seconds, seamless loop.
```

### 文案与互动

- **标题模板**：「碎过的地方，会发光 · No.[编号]」或「建议每个心碎过的人都换上这张壁纸」。
- **正文模板**：一句金缮小知识 + 一句情绪句 + 「高清原图在评论区」。
- **互动钩子**：「评论区告诉我你想修补什么，下一件就做它」。

### 后期要点

- 金线单独提亮并加一点外发光（辉光 5–10%），其余部分压暗。

- 裂纹太密时，直接在对话里说 "Keep everything the same, but reduce the cracks to only four."


## 02 风中织物

栏目口号：**把风穿在身上。** 每周二发布，编号 SILK-001 起。公式固定为"一种颜色 × 一种地貌"，高级时装大片的气质加上辽阔感，是最适合做海报和封面的栏目。

### 视觉规范

| 元素 | 规定 |
| --- | --- |
| 织物 | 单一纯色，长度要"不合理"地长（百米级），占画面 30–50% |
| 地貌 | 单一、空旷、无人工建筑；地貌颜色与织物形成强对比 |
| 人物 | 极小、穿与织物同色的衣服，或完全没有人 |
| 镜头 | 航拍俯视 / 超远景平视二选一，轮流使用 |
| 光线 | 黄金时刻或阴天漫射光，不用正午硬光 |
| 织物形态 | 要有"风的方向"：从一侧吹向另一侧，形成 S 形曲线 |

### 色彩 × 地貌矩阵

从下表任选一组即可生成一篇，对比越强越出图。

| 织物颜色 | 推荐地貌（英文变量） |
| --- | --- |
| crimson 深红 | white sand dunes · a snowfield · a white salt flat |
| pure white chiffon 白纱 | a black volcanic beach · dark basalt cliffs · a stormy grey sea |
| emerald green 祖母绿 | a frozen lake · a red rock canyon |
| marigold yellow 万寿菊黄 | a blue glacier · a lavender field at dusk |
| cobalt blue 钴蓝 | an orange desert · golden wheat fields |
| blush pink 雾粉 | a misty green tea mountain · grey limestone karst |

### 提示词

**壁纸 9:16（航拍版）**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper, aerial top-down drone photograph. An enormous ribbon of [crimson] silk, hundreds of meters long, winds across [white sand dunes] in a graceful S-curve, blown by strong wind from left to right. A tiny figure dressed in [crimson] holds one end; the person is barely visible, less than 2% of the frame. Long soft shadows, golden-hour light, minimal composition, high-fashion editorial mood, cinematic color grading, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

**壁纸 9:16（平视版）**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A vast [black volcanic beach] under an overcast sky. A gigantic sheet of [pure white chiffon] billows high into the air like a frozen wave, many times taller than the tiny figure standing beneath it. Extreme sense of scale, soft diffused light, muted tones, minimalist fine-art photography with a 24mm wide-angle look and film grain. Leave empty sky in the upper third. No text, no watermark.
```

**海报 / 封面 3:4**

```
A vertical 3:4 fashion editorial poster. A model in a flowing [emerald green] silk gown whose train extends endlessly across [a frozen lake], the fabric rising and twisting in the wind. The model is small in the frame. Cool morning light, cinematic, high-end magazine photography. Keep the upper 30% as clean negative space for a title. No text, no watermark.
```

**头像 1:1**

```
A square 1:1 profile picture. A close-up of [crimson] silk rippling in strong wind against [a pale blue sky], abstract flowing folds catching the sunlight, silky sheen, minimal and elegant macro fashion photography. No text, no watermark.
```

### 文案与互动

- **标题模板**：「把 [颜色] 吹进 [地貌] 里」，如「把红色吹进沙漠里」。
- **互动钩子**：「评论区点一个颜色 + 一个地方，点赞最高的下周做」，天然形成 UGC 选题池。
- **合集玩法**：每 6 篇做一次「你是哪种风」心理测试式合集，提高收藏。

### 后期要点

- 织物颜色单独提饱和，地貌降饱和 10–20%，让织物"跳"出来。
- 人物画得太大时，直接对话修改："Keep everything the same, but make the person much smaller, almost a dot."
- 航拍版可以做成"向下慢慢平移"的动态壁纸，用图生视频即可。


## 03 生物荧光

栏目口号：**天黑以后，世界才开始发光。** 每周五晚发布，编号 GLOW-001 起。主打"暗色模式壁纸"：大面积纯黑在 OLED 屏上更省电，发光体对比极强，切换到暗色模式的用户都是目标受众。

### 视觉规范

| 元素 | 规定 |
| --- | --- |
| 背景 | 画面 60% 以上是接近纯黑的暗部 |
| 光色 | 只用冷光：青 #00E5FF、蓝 #2979FF、紫 #B388FF；每张最多两种 |
| 光源 | 只能来自发光生物本身，不出现月亮、路灯等外部光源 |
| 构图 | 壁纸中下部发光、上部留黑，给时钟、通知留位置 |
| 细节 | 漂浮的光粒子是标志元素，每张都要有 |

### 提示词

**壁纸 9:16（森林类）**

```
A vertical 9:16 dark-mode phone wallpaper. [A forest floor at night] covered in bioluminescent [mushrooms and moss] glowing in [electric cyan and soft violet]. Tiny floating light particles drift upward. The glow is the only light source: no moon, no stars, no lamps. The glowing area sits in the lower half; the upper half fades into pure black. Magical, silent atmosphere, long-exposure night photography look, high contrast, ultra detailed, subtle grain. No text, no watermark.
```

**壁纸 9:16（海洋类）**

```
A vertical 9:16 dark-mode phone wallpaper. [Waves breaking on a black sand beach] at night, the water glowing with bioluminescent plankton in [vivid electric blue], with light trails along the shoreline. A starless, pitch-black sky fills the upper half. Long-exposure photography, ultra detailed, dreamy and quiet. No text, no watermark.
```

**头像 1:1**

```
A square 1:1 profile picture. A single [bioluminescent jellyfish] glowing in [cyan and violet], floating in pure black space, with delicate translucent tentacles and tiny light particles around it. Centered composition, macro photography, high contrast. No text, no watermark.
```

**海报 3:4（超现实版）**

```
A vertical 3:4 poster. [Giant glowing jellyfish] drift slowly like lanterns above [a sleeping old town at night], casting soft [blue] light onto the rooftops. All windows are dark. Surreal and peaceful, cinematic wide shot, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

**动效提示词**（图生视频用）

```
Light particles slowly float upward, the glow gently pulses, camera static, 5 seconds, seamless loop.
```

### 文案与互动

- **标题模板**：「适合暗色模式的壁纸 · GLOW-[编号]」「周五夜，给你一片会发光的森林」。

## 04 失重时刻

栏目口号：**把一秒拉长到永远。** 每周三发布，编号 ZERO-001 起。核心是"最有张力的一瞬间被冻结"，是 7 个栏目里最适合做动态壁纸的一个。

### 视觉规范

| 元素 | 规定 |
| --- | --- |
| 动作 | 只用三类：破碎、飞散、溅开 |
| 背景 | 暗调纯色（深灰 #1A1A1A 或墨蓝 #0D1B2A），不出现场景 |
| 色彩 | 整体低饱和 + 唯一一个亮色（红酒、花瓣、橙子等） |
| 光线 | 轮廓光加顶光，让每颗水珠和碎片都有高光 |
| 清晰度 | 所有飞散物必须锐利，不要运动模糊 |

### 动作库

| 动作类型 | 可用主体（英文变量） |
| --- | --- |
| 破碎 shattering | a wine glass of red wine · a porcelain teacup · a glass apple · a perfume bottle · a mirror |
| 飞散 scattering | rose petals · a pearl necklace snapping · feathers · dandelion seeds · cherries |
| 溅开 splashing | milk crown splash · a strawberry dropped into cream · ink dropped in water · an orange sliced in mid-air |
| 人物 figure | a ballerina mid-leap with her dress scattering into petals · long black hair and a white dress floating |

### 提示词

**壁纸 9:16**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper, high-speed photograph at 1/10000 second. [A wine glass of red wine shattering] in mid-air: liquid and glass shards hang weightlessly, every droplet razor-sharp with bright highlights, and [dark red rose petals] float around it. Dark charcoal background with no scenery. Rim light plus a soft top light. The [red wine] is the only saturated color; everything else is muted. Hyperrealistic and cinematic, no motion blur. Keep the upper third mostly empty. No text, no watermark.
```

**人物版 9:16**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A ballerina frozen mid-leap in a dark empty space. Her [white tulle dress] dissolves into hundreds of floating [white petals] scattering into the air, and her hair is suspended weightlessly. Dramatic rim light, deep navy background, high-speed photography look, ethereal and powerful, fine art, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

**头像 1:1**

```
A square 1:1 profile picture. A macro shot of [a strawberry dropped into cream], a frozen crown splash with suspended droplets, perfectly sharp. Dark background, soft top light, centered composition, glossy high-speed food photography. No text, no watermark.
```

### 动态壁纸做法

1. 先用上面的提示词让 GPT Image 生成静图，选最清晰的一张。
2. 丢进可灵 / 即梦的图生视频，运动提示词用：`extreme slow motion, the fragments and droplets drift very slowly, camera static, no new objects appear, 4 seconds`。
3. 用剪映把视频倒放接在正放后面，就是无缝循环的"碎开又复原"。
4. iPhone 实况壁纸需要 Live Photo 格式，可用 intoLive 等工具转换；安卓直接用视频壁纸。
