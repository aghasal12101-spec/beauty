# 02 风中织物

栏目口号：**把风穿在身上。** 每周二发布，编号 SILK-001 起。公式固定为"一种颜色 × 一种地貌"，高级时装大片的气质加上辽阔感，是最适合做海报和封面的栏目。

## 视觉规范

| 元素 | 规定 |
| --- | --- |
| 织物 | 单一纯色，长度要"不合理"地长（百米级），占画面 30–50% |
| 地貌 | 单一、空旷、无人工建筑；地貌颜色与织物形成强对比 |
| 人物 | 极小、穿与织物同色的衣服，或完全没有人 |
| 镜头 | 航拍俯视 / 超远景平视二选一，轮流使用 |
| 光线 | 黄金时刻或阴天漫射光，不用正午硬光 |
| 织物形态 | 要有"风的方向"：从一侧吹向另一侧，形成 S 形曲线 |

## 色彩 × 地貌矩阵

从下表任选一组即可生成一篇，对比越强越出图。

## 提示词

**壁纸 9:16（航拍版）**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper, aerial top-down drone photograph. An enormous ribbon of [crimson] silk, hundreds of meters long, winds across [white sand dunes] in a graceful S-curve, blown by strong wind from left to right. A tiny figure dressed in [crimson] holds one end; the person is barely visible, less than 2% of the frame. Long soft shadows, golden-hour light, minimal composition, high-fashion editorial mood, cinematic color grading, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

**壁纸 9:16（平视版）**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A vast [black volcanic beach] under an overcast sky. A gigantic sheet of [pure white chiffon] billows high into the air like a frozen wave, many times taller than the tiny figure standing beneath it. Extreme sense of scale, soft diffused light, muted tones, minimalist fine-art photography with a 24mm wide-angle look and film grain. Leave empty sky in the upper third. No text, no watermark.
```

**海报 / 封面 3:4**

```
A vertical 3:4 fashion editorial poster. A model in a flowing [emerald green] silk gown whose train extends endlessly across [a frozen lake], the fabric rising and twisting in the wind. The model is small in the frame. Cool morning light, cinematic, high-end magazine photography. Keep the upper 30% as clean negative space for a title. No text, no watermark.
```

**头像 1:1**

```
A square 1:1 profile picture. A close-up of [crimson] silk rippling in strong wind against [a pale blue sky], abstract flowing folds catching the sunlight, silky sheen, minimal and elegant macro fashion photography. No text, no watermark.
```

## 文案与互动

- **标题模板**：「把 [颜色] 吹进 [地貌] 里」，如「把红色吹进沙漠里」。
- **互动钩子**：「评论区点一个颜色 + 一个地方，点赞最高的下周做」，天然形成 UGC 选题池。
- **合集玩法**：每 6 篇做一次「你是哪种风」心理测试式合集，提高收藏。

## 后期要点

- 织物颜色单独提饱和，地貌降饱和 10–20%，让织物"跳"出来。
- 人物画得太大时，直接对话修改："Keep everything the same, but make the person much smaller, almost a dot."
- 航拍版可以做成"向下慢慢平移"的动态壁纸，用图生视频即可。
