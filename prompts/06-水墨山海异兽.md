# 06 水墨山海异兽

栏目口号：**一笔墨，唤醒一只上古异兽。** 每周六发布，编号"兽-壹"起。风格定为**泼墨写意背景 + 工笔细节异兽**：大片留白和晕染的墨里，异兽的眼睛、鳞片、毛发却极其精细。这种"写意与工笔的反差"是惊艳感的来源。

## 视觉规范

| 元素 | 规定 |
| --- | --- |
| 底色 | 生宣纸米白 #F4EFE6，带纸纹纤维 |
| 墨色 | 焦墨 #1A1A1A 到淡墨 #9A9A9A 五层浓淡 |
| 点缀 | 每张只用一处朱砂 #B83A2E：眼睛、尾尖、火焰或印章 |
| 留白 | 画面 40% 以上留白，异兽从墨里"浮现"而不是完整画出 |
| 印章与题字 | 默认不让 AI 写字；需要时只写短词，并逐字核对 |

## 提示词

**壁纸 9:16（泼墨 + 工笔）**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper in the style of a traditional Chinese ink wash painting on aged rice paper. [A nine-tailed fox with flowing silky tails] is rendered in meticulous gongbi detail, with fine brush lines on its fur and eyes, and it emerges from wild splashed-ink (pomo) clouds and mountains. Expressive sumi brush strokes in five tones of black ink, with at least 40% empty negative space. The only color is a single touch of [vermilion] on [its eyes]. Visible paper-fiber texture, ancient and mysterious, museum-masterpiece quality. Monochrome ink apart from the vermilion. No text, no calligraphy, no seal, no watermark.
```

**头像 1:1**

```
A square 1:1 profile picture, traditional Chinese ink wash painting. A portrait of [the head of a dragon with a human face], with detailed gongbi brushwork on the eyes and scales while the rest dissolves into soft ink washes. Rice-paper texture, black ink with a single [vermilion] accent on the eyes, centered composition, minimal and elegant. No text, no seal, no watermark.
```

**图鉴海报 3:4（可让 GPT Image 写异兽名）**

```
A vertical 3:4 poster designed as an ancient bestiary page in traditional Chinese ink painting style. [A one-legged crane-like bird with red markings on a blue-green body and a white beak] stands on a rock, surrounded by faint ink flames. Rice paper with subtle aging stains, black ink with a touch of [vermilion]. On the right side, write the two Chinese characters "[毕方]" vertically in bold black brush calligraphy, with a small red square seal beneath them. No other text, no watermark.
```

> 中文字要逐字核对；生僻字（如"狰""鳐"）出错率更高，出错就改成后期加字。

**动效提示词**（图生视频用）

```
Ink slowly spreads and blooms on rice paper, the creature gradually appears from the ink, camera static, 5 seconds.
```

## 文案与互动

- **标题模板**：「《山海经》里的 [异兽]，用水墨画出来是这样的」。
- **正文**：原文 + 一句白话翻译 + 一个冷知识（如"见则其邑大水"）。知识感会大幅提高收藏率。
- **互动钩子**：「你的守护兽是哪一只？」「评论区点名下一只」。
- **三件套**：同一异兽出壁纸 + 头像 + 图鉴海报，一篇发 3 张，收藏数最高。

## 后期要点

- 叠一层真实宣纸扫描纹理（正片叠底），比 AI 生成的纸纹真实得多。
- 除朱砂外全部去色，避免 AI 混入蓝绿色。
- 题字用竖排方正楷体或行书字体（注意商用授权），配一枚自己的朱红印章做账号标志。
- 参考古画风格可以，但不要照抄现代画师出版的山海经绘本的具体造型。
