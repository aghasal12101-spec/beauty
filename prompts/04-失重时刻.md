# 04 失重时刻

栏目口号：**把一秒拉长到永远。** 每周三发布，编号 ZERO-001 起。核心是"最有张力的一瞬间被冻结"，是 7 个栏目里最适合做动态壁纸的一个。

## 视觉规范

| 元素 | 规定 |
| --- | --- |
| 动作 | 只用三类：破碎、飞散、溅开 |
| 背景 | 暗调纯色（深灰 #1A1A1A 或墨蓝 #0D1B2A），不出现场景 |
| 色彩 | 整体低饱和 + 唯一一个亮色（红酒、花瓣、橙子等） |
| 光线 | 轮廓光加顶光，让每颗水珠和碎片都有高光 |
| 清晰度 | 所有飞散物必须锐利，不要运动模糊 |

## 动作库

| 动作类型 | 可用主体（英文变量） |
| --- | --- |
| 破碎 shattering | a wine glass of red wine · a porcelain teacup · a glass apple · a perfume bottle · a mirror |
| 飞散 scattering | rose petals · a pearl necklace snapping · feathers · dandelion seeds · cherries |
| 溅开 splashing | milk crown splash · a strawberry dropped into cream · ink dropped in water · an orange sliced in mid-air |
| 人物 figure | a ballerina mid-leap with her dress scattering into petals · long black hair and a white dress floating |

## 提示词

**壁纸 9:16**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper, high-speed photograph at 1/10000 second. [A wine glass of red wine shattering] in mid-air: liquid and glass shards hang weightlessly, every droplet razor-sharp with bright highlights, and [dark red rose petals] float around it. Dark charcoal background with no scenery. Rim light plus a soft top light. The [red wine] is the only saturated color; everything else is muted. Hyperrealistic and cinematic, no motion blur. Keep the upper third mostly empty. No text, no watermark.
```

**人物版 9:16**

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A ballerina frozen mid-leap in a dark empty space. Her [white tulle dress] dissolves into hundreds of floating [white petals] scattering into the air, and her hair is suspended weightlessly. Dramatic rim light, deep navy background, high-speed photography look, ethereal and powerful, fine art, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

**头像 1:1**

```
A square 1:1 profile picture. A macro shot of [a strawberry dropped into cream], a frozen crown splash with suspended droplets, perfectly sharp. Dark background, soft top light, centered composition, glossy high-speed food photography. No text, no watermark.
```

## 动态壁纸做法

1. 先用上面的提示词让 GPT Image 生成静图，选最清晰的一张。
2. 丢进可灵 / 即梦的图生视频，运动提示词用：`extreme slow motion, the fragments and droplets drift very slowly, camera static, no new objects appear, 4 seconds`。
3. 用剪映把视频倒放接在正放后面，就是无缝循环的"碎开又复原"。
4. iPhone 实况壁纸需要 Live Photo 格式，可用 intoLive 等工具转换；安卓直接用视频壁纸。

## 文案与互动

- **标题模板**：「1/10000 秒的 [主体]」「这一秒被我冻住了」。
- **互动钩子**：「你想看什么东西碎开的一瞬间？」。
- 动态版和静态版可以拆成两篇发：静态先发测反应，数据好的再做动态版。

## 后期要点

- AI 容易把液体画成"塑料感"，选图时优先水珠有通透感和反光的版本。
- 用局部锐化加强碎片边缘，其他部分保持柔和。
- 除了唯一亮色，其余颜色用 HSL 降饱和。
