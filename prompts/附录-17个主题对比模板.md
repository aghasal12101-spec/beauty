# 附录：17 个主题对比测试模板（GPT Image 版）

用于选题前的对比测试：每个主题用同一画幅（建议 9:16 壁纸）生成 4 张、换 2 组变量，只看原图不做后期，最后填文末的评分表。已选定的 7 个栏目的完整版见各自文件。

## 冰封收藏馆

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A single [red rose] perfectly preserved inside a block of clear glacial ice, with tiny air bubbles and fine fracture lines around it and frost crystals on the surface. A soft museum spotlight from above; deep glacier-blue background fading to darkness. The [red rose] is the only warm color. Quiet, sacred mood, macro photography with shallow depth of field and subtle film grain. Leave the upper third empty. No text, no watermark.
```

变量：`red rose` · `blue morpho butterfly` · `handwritten love letter` · `golden wedding ring` · `red goldfish`

## 1. 巨物

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A tiny lone figure in a red coat stands in [a vast misty wheat field], dwarfed by [a colossal whale floating silently in the sky above]. Extreme sense of scale, low angle, soft overcast light, muted desaturated palette with the red coat as the only accent, atmospheric haze, cinematic wide shot, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

变量：`a calm grey ocean` + `the eyes of a giant stone goddess rising from the water` · `a snowy plain at dusk` + `an enormous full moon touching the horizon`

## 2. 掌中宇宙

```
A vertical 3:4 image. A [porcelain teacup] on a wooden table; inside it is [a miniature stormy ocean with crashing waves and a tiny lighthouse]. Hyper-detailed tilt-shift miniature world, warm side window light, shallow depth of field, whimsical yet realistic macro photography. No text, no watermark.
```

变量：`seashell` + `a tiny glowing city at night` · `perfume bottle` + `a snowstorm over a pine forest`

## 3. 镀金裂痕

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A [white marble bust of a woman] cracked and repaired with glowing gold kintsugi seams. Pure black background, single dramatic side light, high-contrast chiaroscuro, luxurious and melancholic fine-art still life. No text, no watermark.
```

## 4. 液态铬

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A [blooming rose] made entirely of liquid chrome with a mirror-like surface; droplets of molten [silver] drip from the petals. Studio product lighting on a soft [pale grey] gradient background, sharp reflections, futuristic and elegant, hyperrealistic 3D render. No text, no watermark.
```

变量：`butterfly` + `rose gold` · `ribbon bow` + `black chrome`

## 5. 透明之物

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A life-size [horse] sculpted entirely from clear glass stands in [an empty white room]. Sunlight passes through it and casts rainbow caustics on the floor. Transparent with subtle blue edges, minimal and serene fine-art photography. No text, no watermark.
```

变量：`whale` + `a calm shallow sea` · `deer` + `a snowy birch forest`

## 6. 风中织物

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper, aerial view. An enormous flowing piece of [crimson] silk, hundreds of meters long, billows across [white sand dunes], with a tiny figure holding one end. Soft golden-hour light, minimal high-fashion editorial composition, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

## 7. 一色到底

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. A completely [red] world: a [red] horse in a [red] room with [red] walls and a [red] floor. Strictly monochromatic, every object the same hue; only light and shadow create depth. Surreal minimalist set design, soft studio light. No text, no watermark.
```

变量：`cobalt blue` · `butter yellow` · `sage green`

## 8. 红外世界

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. [A winding road through a mountain forest] photographed on infrared false-color film: all foliage is vivid pink and magenta, the sky deep teal. Dreamlike alien atmosphere, soft haze, analog film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

变量：`Lake Bled with its island church` · `the Scottish Highlands with a castle ruin` · `West Lake with a stone bridge`

## 9. 生物荧光

```
A vertical 9:16 dark-mode phone wallpaper. [A forest at night] filled with bioluminescent [mushrooms and moss] glowing in electric cyan and violet, with tiny floating light particles. Deep black shadows; the glow is the only light. Long-exposure night photography, high contrast. No text, no watermark.
```

## 10. 失重时刻

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper, high-speed photograph. [A glass of red wine shattering] in mid-air, liquid and glass shards suspended weightlessly, [rose petals] floating around. Dark background, dramatic rim light, every droplet sharp, cinematic. No text, no watermark.
```

## 11. 倒置之城

```
A vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. [An old European city] mirrored upside down in the sky, two cities facing each other with clouds between them; streets curve and fold upward, and a tiny person walks on [a staircase that leads into the clouds]. Realistic photographic style, soft morning light, ultra-wide angle, dreamlike. No text, no watermark.
```

## 12. 新虚空静物

```
A vertical 3:4 image in the style of a 17th-century Dutch vanitas still-life oil painting. Dark background, dramatic Rembrandt lighting. On a draped table: [a half-finished bubble tea, wireless earbuds, a smartphone showing 1% battery], beside a human skull, a melting candle, an hourglass and wilting tulips. Rich old-master textures, varnished canvas, museum quality. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```

变量：`an alarm clock, an iced americano, a laptop with 99+ unread emails` · `a pile of delivery boxes, a credit card, shopping receipts`
